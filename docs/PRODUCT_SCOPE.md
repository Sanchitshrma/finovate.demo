# Finovate product scope (Stage 0)

## Working decision

Finovate is a paper-trading and investing-education demo. This is the working product direction for the next implementation stages because the current order and price flows are simulated.

## In scope

- Users can practice placing simulated orders with clearly labelled virtual funds.
- Market data is either labelled with its provider and timestamp or explicitly labelled simulated.
- AI research explains company documents and educational material with source citations.

## Out of scope

- No real-money deposits or withdrawals.
- No orders routed to NSE, BSE, MCX, or a broker.
- No claim that Finovate is a registered broker, exchange member, depository participant, investment adviser, or research analyst.
- No unsupported user, client asset, trading-volume, market-leadership, or partner metrics.

## Release rule

Do not enable real-money trading or present Finovate as a brokerage product under this scope. Changing to a real-money product requires a separate legal/compliance and broker-partnership decision before implementing order routing.
